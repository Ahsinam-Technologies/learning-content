# MATH 407 — Stochastic Calculus

**Academic Classification:** Prerequisite for Diffusion & Continuous AI  
**Phase in Master Curriculum:** Φ 07  
**Academic Prerequisites:** MATH 401, MATH 406

## Course Overview
Filtrations, martingales, Brownian motion, Itô integral, Itô lemma, Stochastic Differential Equations (SDEs), Fokker-Planck equation, and score-based generative modeling foundations.
