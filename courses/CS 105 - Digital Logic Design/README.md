# CS 105 — Digital Logic Design

**Academic Classification:** Core Prerequisite  
**Phase in Master Curriculum:** Φ 02  
**Academic Prerequisites:** High School Physics / CS 101  

---

## Course Overview
Boolean algebra, logic gates, combinational and sequential logic, flip-flops, registers, ALUs, and digital circuit fundamentals. Prerequisite for Computer Architecture.

---

## Key Curriculum Pillars
- First-Principles Theoretical & Mathematical Derivations
- Algorithmic Design, Systems Architecture & Proofs
- Problem Sets, Implementations & Benchmark Labs
- Direct Theoretical & Applied Support for Downstream Computing, AI & Systems
