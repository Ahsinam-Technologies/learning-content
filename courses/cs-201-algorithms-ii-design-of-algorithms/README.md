# CS 201 — Algorithms II: Design of Algorithms

**Academic Classification:** Core Sequence  
**Phase in Master Curriculum:** Φ 04  
**Academic Prerequisites:** CS 104  

---

## Course Overview
Advanced algorithm design paradigms: dynamic programming, greedy strategies, network flow, amortized analysis, string matching, and NP-completeness. Direct continuation of Algorithms I and prerequisite for Algorithms III.

---

## Key Curriculum Pillars
- First-Principles Theoretical & Mathematical Derivations
- Algorithmic Design, Systems Architecture & Proofs
- Problem Sets, Implementations & Benchmark Labs
- Direct Theoretical & Applied Support for Downstream Computing, AI & Systems
