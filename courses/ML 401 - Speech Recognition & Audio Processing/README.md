# ML 401 — Speech Recognition & Audio Processing

**Academic Classification:** Core Multimodal  
**Phase in Master Curriculum:** Φ 09  
**Academic Prerequisites:** ML 202, MATH 201  

---

## Course Overview
Acoustic feature extraction (Spectrograms, MFCCs), Hidden Markov Models, CTC loss, neural speech recognition (Whisper, Conformer), text-to-speech, and audio generative models.

---

## Key Curriculum Pillars
- First-Principles Theoretical & Mathematical Derivations
- Algorithmic Design, Systems Architecture & Proofs
- Problem Sets, Implementations & Benchmark Labs
- Direct Theoretical & Applied Support for Downstream Computing, AI & Systems
