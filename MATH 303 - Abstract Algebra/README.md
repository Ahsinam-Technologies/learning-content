# MATH 303 — Abstract Algebra

**Academic Classification:** Core Math  
**Phase in Master Curriculum:** Φ 05  
**Academic Prerequisites:** MATH 104  

---

## Course Overview
Groups, subgroups, homomorphisms, Lagrange theorem, quotient groups, rings, ideals, integral domains, and fields. Underpins symmetries in geometric DL and cryptography.
