# CS 309 — Information Theory

**Academic Classification:** Prerequisite for ML Loss Functions & VAEs  
**Phase in Master Curriculum:** Φ 07  
**Academic Prerequisites:** MATH 105, CS 101  

---

## Course Overview
Shannon entropy, joint & conditional entropy, mutual information, Kullback-Leibler (KL) divergence, cross-entropy, channel capacity, rate-distortion theory, and asymptotic equipartition property.

---

## Key Curriculum Pillars
- First-Principles Theoretical & Mathematical Derivations
- Algorithmic Design, Systems Architecture & Proofs
- Problem Sets, Implementations & Benchmark Labs
- Direct Theoretical Support for Downstream Machine Learning, Deep Learning & Cloud Systems
